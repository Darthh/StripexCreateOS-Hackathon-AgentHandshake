    Payment Methods

    Payment Types

    We offer two flexible payment approaches:

    1. Subscription-Based Payment

    • Choose a monthly tier (Free, Beginner, Pro, or Enterprise)
    • Receive initial monthly credits based on your tier
    • Bonus: Top-ups within subscription tiers receive multipliers
      • Free Tier: 1.2x credits on top-up
      • Beginner Tier: 1.2x credits on top-up
      • Pro Tier: 1.5x credits on top-up
      • Enterprise Tier: 5x credits on top-up
    • If monthly credits are exhausted, you can top-up with the multiplier bonus

    2. Pure Top-Up Payment

    • No monthly subscription required
    • Pay only for what you use
    • Credits purchased at standard rate (no multiplier)
    • Full flexibility with no recurring commitments

    Accepted Payment Methods

    We accept the following payment options with auto-pay available:

    • Fiat Currency: Traditional payment via credit card, bank transfer, etc.
    • Cryptocurrency: Pay with your preferred crypto assets
    • NODE Token: Native platform token payments

    All payment methods support automatic payment (auto-pay) for seamless credit management.

    Credit Management

    AI Credits (Create)

    Initial credits are allocated specifically for the Create feature. Here's how it works:

    1. Initial Allocation: Each tier receives initial AI credits as shown in the tier structure
    2. Credit Exhaustion: If your AI credits run out but you still have Deploy credits remaining
    3. Credit Transfer: You can transfer Deploy credits to top-up your AI credits
    4. Custom Limits: Set your own consumption limits based on your usage patterns

    Credit Limit Management

    You have full control over your credit usage:

    • Monitor consumption across all services
    • Set custom spending limits per service
    • Transfer credits between pools as needed
    • Increase credit limits based on your requirements
    • Real-time credit balance tracking

    Billing Model

    All billing is consumption-based:

    • You're only charged for actual resource usage
    • Billing calculated per second for CPU, RAM, and Disk
    • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
    • Detailed usage reports available

    Getting Started

    1. Choose Your Tier: Select a subscription tier or opt for pure top-up
    2. Select Payment Method: Set up fiat, crypto, or NODE token payments
    3. Enable Auto-Pay (Optional): Never run out of credits automatically
    4. Set Limits for Create: Configure consumption limits to control spending
    5. Start Building: Begin using the platform with your allocated credits
